## 3. Coral button boundary (Mist)

The coral fill measures 2.82:1 against a white Mist card as an *edge*. Resolution recorded in `DECISIONS_AND_DELTAS.md §5`: the button is identified by its 6.06:1 label, and WCAG 1.4.11 does not require a boundary contrast when the control is otherwise identified; in addition every primary button in this package carries a 1px `border-foreground/25` hairline in Mist (`portal-btn` recipe), measured below, so the edge is also visible on white.

Primary button hairline: width 1px; hairline vs white card **4.22:1**; hairline vs coral fill 1.50:1.
