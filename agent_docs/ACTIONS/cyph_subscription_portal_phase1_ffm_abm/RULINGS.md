## R-008 — Temporary access rule: admin OR superadmin (Director ruling)
- **ID / date / seat:** R-008 · 27 October 2026 (Asia/Dhaka) · recorded by Claudy (Engineer); decision is the Director's.
- **Approving source:** Tony's chat dispatch, 27 Oct 2026: "pause the broader E2 build. Fix admin login first." … "Existing admin accounts must be able to log in and open /dashboard. Retain existing superadmin access for now. Member accounts remain denied access to account management. Public registration remains public under the previous correction. This supersedes the earlier instruction to deny superadmins."
- **Original observation:** the inherited foundation was superadmin-only in three places: the `LoginPageContent` role check (signed admins back out), `getSuperAdminUser()` used by `(portal)/layout.tsx`, and the `/login` page redirect. The Shell foot label was hard-coded "Super Admin". `/dashboard` had no page-level guard, so a signed-out 307 body still streamed the page's RSC payload (pre-existing).
- **Record gap (stated, not resolved):** this module's records hold no earlier "deny superadmins" instruction and no "public registration" correction. R-008 quotes both as the Director stated them; nothing here re-derives them.
- **Exact decision:** account management (the `(portal)` group, currently `/dashboard`) admits `user_roles.role` ∈ {`admin`, `superadmin`}. `member`, no role row, and signed-out are denied (→ `/login`; the login form signs a member back out). The role comes only from `public.user_roles` through the existing `getUserRole()`/session machinery; `user_metadata` is never a role source. No DB role change, account creation, auth bypass, or Mission Control/OwedBook change.
- **Implementation:** `src/services/auth/access.ts` (pure `isPortalStaffRole`), `types/auth.ts` `SuperAdminUser` → `PortalStaffUser {role}`, `services/auth/session.ts` `getSuperAdminUser` → `getPortalStaffUser`, `(portal)/layout.tsx`, `login/page.tsx`, `login/LoginPageContent.tsx`, `components/portal/Shell.tsx` role label, `(portal)/dashboard/page.tsx` page guard. Tests: `src/__tests__/portal-access.test.tsx`, `src/__tests__/login-form.test.tsx`.
- **Temporary:** applies until a later Director ruling defines customer (owner/store-admin/member) authorization. Synthetic demo personas are unaffected: this rule is about the real staff login, not the mock owner/admin/member fixtures.
- **Affected ACs/files:** AC-01 (the "actual authenticated non-superadmin remains denied" clause now reads "actual authenticated member remains denied; admin and superadmin enter"), AC-02 (login behaviour); EXECUTION_PLAN §1/§4.1/§8 X1/§9 B3; QAM handoff.
- **Required regression:** admin and superadmin reach `/dashboard`; member and role-less are denied at the login form, the layout and the page; signed-out `/dashboard` → 307 `/login` with no page payload; a forged `user_metadata` role, query or cookie does not grant access.
- **Resulting contract version:** ACCEPTANCE_SPEC v1.0 frozen text kept; R-008 amendment pointer appended under AC-01.
- **Verification record (added 27 Oct 2026, Director instruction):** after the R-008 fix, Tony signed in with his own existing admin account at the Engineer-started local app (`http://localhost:3001/login`) and reported "admin login works". This is **Director-reported manual verification**, not an Engineer-executed browser test: no automated real-credential login was run by Engineering, and the role value of Tony's account was not observed by Engineering. Engineering evidence for R-008 is limited to mocked-role unit tests, mutation checks, a production build and signed-out route probes.
