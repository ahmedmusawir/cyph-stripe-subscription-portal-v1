All three of Cody's findings reproduced exactly on the current candidate before any change. All three are repaired. **This is engineering evidence, not QA certification.** R3-F01, R3-F02 and R3-O03 stay open until Cody retests.

**What was wrong**
- **R3-F01:** the v3 migration treated any *open* stored checkout as unedited. That holds for R-012, which closes an open checkout on any edit. It doesn't hold for R-011, which kept the checkout open and only dropped the quote. So Cody's R-011 record (1 store, $99, then edited to 2 stores) was bound to 2 stores at $148 and could be paid.
- **R3-F02:** activation took names and amount from the paid details but took the nominated admin from the live draft. So it said "a separate store invitation went to Replacement Admin" and offered that admin's preview, although the payment never covered them. The email previews also read the draft.
- **R3-O03:** in dark mode, the focus ring on "Use the details I paid for" measured 2.99:1 against the warning tint around it. The inner side, against its offset, was fine at 4.37:1.

**What changed**
- **A stored checkout gets bound only when the record itself proves what it was for:**
  - it still has the quote it opened with (same quote, same draft revision, same store count), and that quote's price is used; or
  - it still exactly matches its demo seed.
- Every R-011 and R-012 edit drops the quote, so an edited record never qualifies. Nothing falls back to a recomputed price any more.
- **A record that can't be proven stays held:**
  - no scope is invented, nothing confirms and nothing can be restored;
  - no second checkout can open;
  - the screen says "We can't match this payment to your current details. Please don't pay again", with a support reference. The stand-in no longer says "after you paid" for these.
  - For an unknown payment, "Stripe confirms nothing charged" still works, followed by a fresh quote and a new key.
- **Safe migrations still bind:** an unedited legacy checkout pays $99.00 at its own quote price, discounts included. Seeds bind while unchanged, and v1 records bind before their quote is dropped. Paid legacy records now bind by the same rule.
- **Completed activation and the email previews read what the payment covered.** An admin added afterwards is never named or previewed. An admin who was covered stays the invited one. Payment history is untouched.
- **Alerts get a slightly lighter focus ring in dark mode only.** The restore button now measures 3.33:1 against the tint (was 2.99) and 4.86:1 against its offset. Light mode, the global colour token and keyboard behaviour are unchanged.
