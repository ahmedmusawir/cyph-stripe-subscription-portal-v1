Both of Cody's failures reproduced exactly on the current candidate, and both are repaired in the mock service and the UI. **This is not QA certification.** R2-F01 and R2-F02 stay open until Cody retests.

**What was wrong**
- **R2-F01:** R-012 deliberately keeps a submitted (processing or unknown) payment open when details change, but nothing recorded what that payment was for. So it confirmed whatever the draft had become: for example a 2-store or renamed signup under the original 1-store $99 payment.
- **R2-F02:** the checkout service only treated a key as "in use" while its checkout was pending. A closed checkout's key could open a new checkout, and resuming forgot the key entirely.

**What changed**
- **Each checkout now records what it was quoted for:** store count, pharmacies, discount code and price. That record never changes.
- **A processing or unknown payment can only confirm that scope.** If the details changed afterwards, Refresh status and "Stripe confirms payment" are held: the service refuses them, not just the buttons. The screen says "Your details changed after you paid" and names what the payment covers, e.g. "1 pharmacy (Example Main Street Pharmacy), $99.00".
- **The way forward never charges again:**
  - **"Use the details I paid for"** puts the details back, and the payment then confirms exactly that.
  - For an unknown payment, **"Stripe confirms nothing charged"** still works. Then the edited details get a fresh quote ($148.00 for 2 stores), fresh consent and a new checkout with a new key.
  - A second checkout still can't start while one is in flight.
- **Activation shows the paid amount and pharmacies,** not the edited ones.
- **Keys from closed checkouts are refused** by the service. That covers changed details, a withdrawn declaration, back, declined, nothing charged, resume and restart after expiry. Same-key replay of an open checkout is unchanged. The review page now gets a new key once no checkout is open, so paying again on the same page still works.
- **Saved demo state moves to storage version 3.** An older saved checkout is bound to its details only if they provably haven't changed since payment. Otherwise it's held, with "We can't match this payment…, don't pay again" and a pointer to support.
