**What was wrong (one cause)**
Two readers trusted the activation flag on its own, and old builds could leave it disagreeing with the checkout record:
- **R4-F01:** "nothing charged" closed the checkout but left a pending activation behind. Refresh saw "pending" and confirmed it. A terminal no-charge became paid.
- **R4-F02:** resume accepted "activation failed" as a reason to discard the record, even when the checkout was still `unknown`. A payment Stripe hadn't answered was thrown away, and a second checkout opened.
- Refresh also returned a silent "success" on a failed flag, so stale calls looked fine.

**What changed**
- **One precedence rule, `paymentPhase`:** the checkout record governs; the activation flag counts only where it agrees with it.
  - paid → never reversed, restarted or replayed
  - unknown → Stripe's answer is still owed, whatever old flag is attached
  - processing → pending checkout and pending activation agree
  - failed → declined checkout with a recorded failure
  - not charged → abandoned or expired; a stale flag means nothing
- **Service:** Refresh advances only a processing payment, or a paid one still being set up. Resume needs an abandoned or declined checkout. "Nothing charged" now clears the activation, so nothing is left to refresh. Every refusal is a conflict with the state unchanged.
- **Screen:** Activation renders from the phase. Unknown shows "We're still checking…" with a link to checkout status and no retry or refresh. Not charged shows "Nothing was charged for this payment" with "Continue my signup". "Try payment again" appears only for a genuine failure.
- **Stored records are not rewritten:** no storage bump. Conflicting old flags stay as history, and the readers apply the same precedence to old and new records.
- **Unchanged:** genuine confirmation and failure, unknown → paid / nothing charged, fresh quote and new key after a closed checkout, scope binding, restore, spent keys, owner declaration, completed history.
