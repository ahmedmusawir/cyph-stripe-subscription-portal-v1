**What was wrong**

With a stale flag it said "Waiting…" plus Refresh, or "Payment didn't go through" plus "Try payment again". - **R5-F02:** resend looked only at the activation flags, never the checkout. A stale "confirmed, set up" flag let it mark the email sent on no checkout, an unknown one, or a closed unpaid one.

**What changed**
- **One agreement check:** the activation record counts only where it matches the payment phase (processing ↔ pending, failed ↔ failed, paid ↔ confirmed).
- **Activation page reads the phase first:**
  - paid with a missing or conflicting activation → "Payment confirmed" and "We can't show setup or access-email status here", with the paid amount when recorded and a support reference. No setup or email claim, no Refresh, retry or resend.
  - nothing charged (including the real path) → its terminal notice and "Continue my signup".
  - unknown → unchanged notice; no checkout or open checkout → "There's no payment to track yet".
- **Resend:** only for a paid checkout whose recorded setup finished. Everything else is refused, with nothing changed. Valid paid resend, from a sent or a failed email, works as before.
- **The contract read `readActivation`** follows the same rule.
- **R5-O03 (left alone):** repeating Refresh after setup finished still returns OK with an identical copy of the state. Per the Lead it's a nonblocking no-op, so Cody's identity assertion still fails and I report it as such.
