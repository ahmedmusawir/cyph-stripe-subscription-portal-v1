**3. Repair semantics**
- **Open checkouts are closed when their basis changes.** An unpaid checkout with no submitted payment closes when:
  - the declaration is withdrawn (Continue or Back),
  - a v1 draft is restored, or
  - the draft changes materially.

  The record becomes `abandoned` with a `closedReason`. Nothing is charged, the details are kept and the record is not erased.
- **Every move toward payment re-checks the current draft**, in the service and not only the UI: same-key replay, paid/processing, unknown→paid and activation refresh. A failure leaves state unchanged.
- **A payment that was already submitted is held, not cancelled.** It continues only after explicit re-acceptance. "Nothing charged", back and decline stay available.
- **Recovery is explicit.** The owner ticks the declaration, then the review screen shows "we closed that checkout". Get a fresh quote, give fresh consent, and a new checkout opens with a new operation key. Re-ticking never reopens the old checkout or restores consent. Paid history is never touched.
- **CR-F02:** pharmacy entries are created only as each step is filled in, and steps fill in order. No business cap was added; the only bound is still the safe-integer pricing bound. R2-N02 is fixed. The 21-store journey completes at $1,079.00.
- **O04 residual:** the plan Actions column has the approved divider above it on mobile and beside it on desktop, with 12 px gaps. Nothing else was redesigned.
